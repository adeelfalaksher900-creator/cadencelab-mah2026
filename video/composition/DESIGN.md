# CadenceLab visual identity
## Style Prompt
An editorial audio-analysis workbench: warm paper surfaces, strong typographic hierarchy, fine grid lines, restrained vermilion highlights, and large readable speech timelines. Serious enough for a debate coach, approachable enough for a student. The recording is the hero; evidence is always one click away.
## Colors
- Paper #F8F6F1
- Ink #202422
- Secondary ink #626760
- Accent #B7482D
- Teal reference #247D79
## Typography
Georgia display headings; DM Sans interface; monospace timings.
## Motion
Short 160–240ms state transitions, reduced-motion support. Video entrances ease power2.out or sine.out. No infinite animation. No decorative graphs or fabricated live metrics.
## What NOT to Do
No purple gradients, glassmorphism, score confetti, universal "good speaker" claims, unreadable tiny labels, or default dashboard card grids.
